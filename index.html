Transparency on this question is a signal of maturity, not a liability. Here is our honest answer.

Our own infrastructure

To date, Kodeks has not experienced a security incident resulting in unauthorized access to client data or systems. We operate under a security-first posture internally: the same tooling and controls we deploy for clients (SentinelOne EDR, Fortinet firewalls, Wazuh SIEM, encrypted backups with tested recovery) are applied to our own environment.

Client environments we manage

In the course of managing client infrastructure over 20 years, our team has responded to and contained security incidents on behalf of clients, including ransomware events, unauthorized access attempts, and network intrusions. In each case, our incident response process followed a structured sequence:

  • Immediate containment: isolation of affected systems within the P1 SLA window
  • Forensic preservation: evidence capture before any remediation activity
  • Root cause analysis: documented findings shared with the client within 48 hours of containment
  • Remediation: patching, reconfiguration, and access review
  • Post-incident review: written report covering timeline, impact, root cause, and control improvements

We do not discuss the specifics of client incidents publicly. If a reference client is willing to speak about their experience of our incident response, we can facilitate that introduction under mutual NDA.

What we have never done

Concealed a breach from an affected client, delayed notification beyond the contractually agreed window, or attributed an incident to client error without documented evidence.

Data Processing Agreement

We provide a standard Data Processing Agreement as part of every engagement that involves access to or processing of client personal data. The DPA covers:

  • Subject matter, nature, and purpose of processing
  • Categories of data subjects and types of personal data
  • Our obligations as a data processor under GDPR Article 28
  • Sub-processor disclosure and change notification process
  • Data subject rights support procedures
  • Breach notification timeline (72-hour notification to the client)
  • Data deletion and return procedures on contract termination

Clients who prefer to use their own DPA template are welcome to do so. We will engage legal review within five business days of receipt.

We can also support clients preparing Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) where our services form part of a higher-risk processing activity.

We maintain a documented subprocessor list and will provide the current version on request or as an annex to your DPA. Subprocessors are organizations that process personal data on our behalf in the course of delivering services to you.

Your data remains yours. Upon contract termination, we follow a structured data exit procedure that is documented in your MSA and DPA:

  • Within 30 days of the termination date, we will provide a full export of any client data held on Kodeks-managed infrastructure, in an agreed format (encrypted archive, cloud storage transfer, or physical media, depending on volume and sensitivity).
  • Following confirmation of successful data receipt by the client, all copies of client data held on Kodeks systems, including backups, logs, and any secondary copies, are securely deleted within a further 30 days.
  • We will provide a written certificate of deletion upon request, confirming the scope and method of data destruction.
  • Where regulatory obligations require us to retain certain records (e.g. audit logs relevant to a security incident under investigation), we will notify you of the specific data, the legal basis for retention, and the retention period.
