ADVERSARIAL SECURITY ASSESSMENT

Find the way in before someone less friendly does.

Our penetration testing service simulates real-world attacks against your infrastructure, applications, and people, using OWASP Testing Guide, NIST SP 800-115, and MITRE ATT&CK methodologies, the same techniques credentialed adversaries use, aligned with ISO 27001 and GDPR. You get a prioritised remediation plan your team can act on this quarter, plus an executive-grade summary your board can act on next.

01
Planning & scoping
Rules of engagement, permitted actions, sign-off. No test runs without written authorisation.
02
Reconnaissance
Passive (OSINT) and active surface mapping. Inventory of attack-relevant assets.
03
Vulnerability identification
Automated scanning plus manual review across networks, services, applications, and authentication.
04
Controlled exploitation
Privilege escalation, lateral movement, and data access - simulated, contained, fully reversible.
05
Analysis & reporting
Dual-format report: technical with reproducible findings, executive with risk-rated remediation roadmap.
